You are reading this notice because a company that uses {{product.name}} (the "customer") checks payment requests in which you appear: as an employee whose salary account is being changed, as a contact person of one of its vendors, or as the sender of an e-mail that was forwarded for checking. This notice explains what is recorded about you, why, for how long and how you can exercise your rights.

## 1. Who is responsible

- **The customer is the controller** (in Russia, the operator) of your data in {{product.name}}. It decides which payment requests are checked, who in its organisation can see them and how long the records are kept within the limits below. Contact the customer first, for example through the finance contact named in the message you received.
- **{{company.name}} is the processor.** We provide the software and process your data only on the customer's instructions under a data processing agreement. If you contact us at {{company.privacy_email}}, we forward your request to the customer and help it answer.

## 2. What is recorded

| If you are              | What may be recorded                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| An employee             | your name; the request to change your salary account and the account details (stored encrypted and shown masked to the last four digits); the alert sent to you about the change and your answer ("yes, it was me" or "no, it was not me") |
| A vendor contact        | your name, role and phone number when the customer records you as a callback contact; the date and outcome of verification calls made to you, the number dialled and your name as the person spoken to, and the caller's notes             |
| The sender of an e-mail | your e-mail address, display name and domain; the message text, subject and attachments forwarded for checking; technical results such as whether the message passed sender authentication                                                 |

No audio of calls is recorded. Phone numbers and bank details are stored encrypted. They never appear in full in notifications, logs or texts sent to AI models; notifications show at most the last four digits.

## 3. Why

To prevent payment fraud: before the customer pays, it checks that a change of bank details or a payment request really comes from you, by calling you on a number it already trusts and keeping a record of the check. In the EU and the UK the customer relies on its legitimate interest in preventing fraud and protecting its funds and, for employees, on the performance of the employment contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) and (b)). In Russia the customer relies on the grounds of Article 6(1) of Federal Law No. 152-FZ, in particular the performance of a contract and the exercise of its rights and legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(5) and (7)).

## 4. Who can see it

Only members of the customer's workspace, according to their roles (for example the finance team and the approvers). Full bank details can be revealed only by members with a dedicated permission, and each reveal is recorded. The data is never shared with other customers of {{product.name}}. If the customer enables the Telegram bot, notifications to its members contain only masked data.

## 5. How long it is kept

- Uploaded files and the text of e-mails: up to 90, 730 or 2555 days depending on the customer's plan (at least 30 days), after which they are deleted and only masked details of the payment request remain.
- Records of verification calls and decisions: as part of the customer's evidence ledger, {{retention.scampause_evidence}} days (7 years) by default; the customer may set between 5 and 10 years.
- Payroll alerts and approval notifications: {{retention.scampause_notifications}} days.

## 6. Your rights

You can ask the customer to give you access to your data, to correct it, to erase it, to restrict its processing or to stop processing it, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interests. The customer can find, export or redact your data in {{product.name}}: redaction removes your name and phone number as a contact, the name of the person spoken to, the number dialled and the notes, and your e-mail address from stored messages, while the hash-chained evidence of decisions remains verifiable. You can also complain to your data protection supervisory authority (in Russia, Roskomnadzor).

## 7. Payroll alerts

Payroll change alerts are security messages. They deliberately contain no unsubscribe link, because such a link would let a fraudster silence them. To object to receiving them, contact the finance contact named in the alert.
