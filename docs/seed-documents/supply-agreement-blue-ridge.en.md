# Supply Agreement No. BRP-2025-014 (excerpt)

**Supplier:** Blue Ridge Packaging Company, Inc., 410 Industrial Park Drive, Asheville, NC, USA ("Supplier"), trading as Blue Ridge Packaging Co.
**Buyer:** Northwind Coffee Roasters LLC ("Buyer")
**Effective date:** 15 September 2025 · **Term:** 24 months, renewing automatically for 12-month periods

*This excerpt contains the sections relevant to invoicing, payments and official contacts. The signed original is kept by the Finance Lead.*

## 4. Products and orders
4.1 Supplier provides printed coffee bags (250 g and 1 kg, valve and zip), shipping cartons and labels under purchase orders issued by Buyer.
4.2 Only purchase orders sent from an @northwindroasters.com address and signed by the Owner or the Finance Lead are valid.

## 7. Prices and invoicing
7.1 Prices are fixed in Schedule A for 12 months. Any change requires 60 days' written notice.
7.2 Supplier issues one invoice per delivery, referencing the purchase order number. Invoices are sent from billing@blueridgepack.com only.
7.3 Payment terms: net 30 days from the delivery date. Supplier will not ask Buyer to pay earlier than the due date and will not demand
same-day payment.

## 8. Payment
8.1 Buyer pays by ACH transfer to Supplier's account specified in Schedule B (JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., account ending 4417, account
holder "Blue Ridge Packaging Company, Inc.").
8.2 Supplier does not accept payment by card, cryptocurrency, gift cards or to accounts held outside the United States.

## 9. Notices, official contacts and changes to payment instructions
9.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by courier or e-mail to the addresses in this section.
9.2 **Official contact for billing and payment confirmation (Supplier):** Karen Whitfield, Accounts Receivable Manager,
telephone **+1 828 555 0142**, e-mail karen.whitfield@blueridgepack.com. Buyer may call this number at any time during business hours
(Monday–Friday, 08:00–17:00 Eastern Time) to confirm an invoice or payment instruction.
9.3 **Changes to payment instructions.** Supplier will notify any change of its bank account only by a letter on Supplier's letterhead
signed by its Chief Financial Officer. A change becomes effective only after Buyer has confirmed it by a telephone call **initiated by
Buyer to the number in clause 9.2**. Supplier will never request a change of bank details by e-mail alone, and an e-mail asking Buyer not to
call is not a valid notice.
9.4 If Buyer pays to an account other than the one in Schedule B because of instructions that were not confirmed under clause 9.3, the
payment does not discharge Buyer's obligation; however, the parties will cooperate in good faith to recover the funds.
9.5 **Official contact (Buyer):** Maria Kovaleva, Finance Lead, ap@northwindroasters.com.

## 12. Term and termination
12.1 Either party may terminate with 90 days' written notice.
