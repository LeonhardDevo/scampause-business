# Vendor Master Register

**Company:** Northwind Coffee Roasters LLC · **Maintained by:** Maria Kovaleva (Finance Lead) · **Reviewed:** quarterly

This register lists the **official callback contacts** for our regular payees and a masked summary of their payment details.
The live, authoritative data is in ScamPause (Vendors & payees); this document is the printable reference for audits.
Only the last four digits of accounts are shown. Full numbers are visible in ScamPause to authorised staff and every reveal is logged.

## How to use the callback contacts

- Use these numbers for "Stop. Call. Confirm." — they come from contracts, verified websites or in-person meetings.
- Never replace a number here with one taken from an invoice or e-mail. Changes to this register need the Finance Lead and a source document.
