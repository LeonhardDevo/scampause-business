# Payment Approval Policy

**Company:** Northwind Coffee Roasters LLC
**Policy owner:** Alex Morgan, Owner
**Maintained by:** Maria Kovaleva, Finance Lead
**Version:** 2.1, effective 1 September 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy makes sure that no single person can send company money on the strength of an e-mail, a phone call or a document alone.
Every outgoing payment is prepared by one person and approved by at least one other person. Payments that look different from usual
(new bank details, new vendor, urgent requests, payroll changes) get an independent callback check first: **Stop. Call. Confirm.**

## 2. Scope

The policy applies to every outgoing payment made from company bank accounts: supplier invoices, contractor invoices, utilities, leases,
payroll changes, refunds, and any "urgent" transfer requested by anyone, including the Owner. It also applies to our external bookkeepers
(Ledgerline Bookkeeping) when they prepare payments on our behalf.

## 3. Approval thresholds

All amounts are in US dollars. Payments in other currencies are converted at the exchange rate recorded in ScamPause for the invoice date;
if no rate is recorded, the strictest rule applies.

| Situation | Approvers required | Who | Callback check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Every payment | 1 | Owner, Finance Lead or Operations Manager — never the person who prepared it | Only if another row requires it |
| Amount from **$3,000** | **2 approvers** (dual control) | Any two eligible approvers | Only if another row requires it |
| Amount from **$10,000** | 2 approvers | **One of them must be the Owner** | Only if another row requires it |
| **Change of a vendor's bank details**, or payment to an account we have not used with that vendor before | 2 approvers | One must be the Owner or the Finance Lead | **Required** before approval |
| **Payroll account change** for an employee | 2 approvers | One must be the Owner | **Required** — call the employee on the number in the HR file, or confirm in person |
| ScamPause shows **high or critical risk** | 2 approvers | Any two eligible approvers | **Required** |
| First payment to a new vendor of **$1,000 or more** | as per amount | — | **Required** |

Example: an invoice of $4,000 from any vendor needs two approvers. An invoice of $12,500 needs two approvers and one of them must be the Owner.

## 4. Approval limits

| Person | Role | Limit per payment | Monthly limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alex Morgan | Owner | no limit | no limit |
| Maria Kovaleva | Finance Lead (admin) | no limit | no limit |
| Sam Patel | Operations Manager (approver) | $25,000 | $60,000 |
| Irina Sokolova (Ledgerline Bookkeeping) | Bookkeeper | cannot approve | — |

An approver who has reached a limit is skipped automatically and another approver is assigned.

## 5. Segregation of duties

1. The person who requested or prepared a payment cannot approve it.
2. The person who performed the callback check can be at most one of the two approvers.
3. The person who added a bank account to the vendor master cannot mark it as verified.
4. Bookkeepers prepare, verify and record payments but never approve them.

## 6. Deadlines, reminders and escalation

- Approvers have **48 hours** to decide. If the deadline falls outside business hours (Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 New York time),
  it moves to 12:00 on the next business day.
- ScamPause sends reminders after **4 hours** and **24 hours**, and a final reminder 2 hours before the deadline. No reminders are sent
  between 21:00 and 08:00.
- If nobody decides within **24 hours**, the request is escalated to the Owner.
- An expired request is **not approved**. It can be reopened from the case file; if the callback check is older than 7 days, it must be repeated.

## 7. Approving in Telegram

Approvers may approve in Telegram only when the risk level is low or medium and the bank details have not changed.
Everything else must be approved in the ScamPause web app after reviewing the evidence. Rejecting is allowed in Telegram at any time.

## 8. "Urgent" requests

Urgency is never a reason to skip this policy. Requests that say "urgent", "today", "confidential" or "don't call me" get **more**
scrutiny, not less. Nobody at Northwind — including the Owner — will ever ask anyone to bypass approvals.

## 9. After approval

Approval in ScamPause means our people approved the payment after the required checks. The payment itself is made in our bank by the
Finance Lead or the bookkeeper, who then marks it as paid in ScamPause with the bank reference. ScamPause shows risk signals; it does not
guarantee that a payment is legitimate.

## 10. Records

Verification and approval evidence is kept for 7 years. Evidence packets (PDF) are generated automatically for every decided request.

Ask the Finance Lead (Maria Kovaleva) or use the ScamPause assistant: for example, "Do we need a second approver for $4,000?".
